Same Pill, Different Person

Why can the same dose of celecoxib affect people differently?

I’m looking at how CYP2C9 variants *2 and *3 change celecoxib metabolism. I compared variant frequencies across five ancestry groups using PharmGKB, gnomAD, and CPIC, then translated the findings into evidence-based dosing recommendations.

FROM PEAS TO PROBABILITY / 18-PAGE IA
05
MATHEMATICS IA · 2026

From Peas to Probability

Do Mendel’s predicted ratios hold in a large sample?

I tested the 3:1 and 9:3:3:1 ratios on more than 15,900 pea plant offspring using three statistical methods. None showed a significant deviation (all p > 0.6).

METHODS Chi-square · statistical analysis · probability
