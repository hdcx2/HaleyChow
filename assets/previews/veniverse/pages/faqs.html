Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guidance for using The Veniverse and finding reliable information

Are vascular tumors and malformations the same?

No. They are separate groups in the ISSVA classification. Ask a vascular anomalies clinician which term applies to a diagnosis.

See the ISSVA classification

Can every malformation be treated the same way?

No. Care depends on the type, flow, location, symptoms, and individual medical situation. A specialist team can explain options and risks.

Read the treatment overview

Does a genetic diagnosis always mean it was inherited?

No. Some changes are inherited, some arise for the first time, and some are present only in affected tissue. The answer depends on the condition and test result.

Learn about inheritance

How do I find a specialist?

Start with a multidisciplinary program or the ISSVA directories, then confirm the team’s experience with the exact condition and anatomy.

Open the specialist directory

Can I use this website to make care decisions?

No. This website is for education and resource navigation, not diagnosis or personalized treatment advice. Discuss questions with a qualified clinician.

Where are the CAD and 3D models?

The CAD and interactive model areas are separate projects and are not included in this round of content updates.
