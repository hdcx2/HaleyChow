Clinical Trials

Search the official U.S. study registry and discuss results with a clinician

ClinicalTrials.gov is a searchable registry, not a recommendation or guarantee of access. Study status and eligibility can change. Each record’s sponsor and study team are responsible for confirming whether someone may participate.

How to search

Check the study status, location, age range, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and contact details. You can filter for recruiting studies and by location.

ClinicalTrials.gov: patient search help

Before enrolling: Review the study with the treating clinician and contact the listed research team. Participation is voluntary; this website does not assess eligibility.
