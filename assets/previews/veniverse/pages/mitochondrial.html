Mitochondrial Disorders

A group of conditions that can affect how cells make energy

Mitochondrial diseases are a varied group of metabolic disorders. They can involve gene changes in mitochondrial DNA or nuclear DNA, and inheritance patterns and affected body systems differ by condition. These pages are starting points; a genetics or metabolic specialist can help interpret a specific diagnosis.
