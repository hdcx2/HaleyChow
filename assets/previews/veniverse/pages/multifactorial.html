Multifactorial / Complex Disorders

How many genes and non-genetic factors can contribute to health

Complex or multifactorial conditions do not usually follow a single-gene inheritance pattern. Many genetic and environmental influences can contribute, and family history alone cannot predict an individual’s diagnosis or outcome.

How complex risk works

Some common conditions reflect combined effects of many gene variants and factors such as environment and lifestyle. “Runs in a family” does not by itself reveal the cause or a person’s level of risk.

Genetic counseling

A genetics professional can help interpret family history and test results and explain what a result can—and cannot—tell relatives. Ask a primary or specialty clinician about referral options.
