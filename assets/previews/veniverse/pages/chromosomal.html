Chromosomal Disorders

Conditions caused by changes in chromosome number or structure

Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21)

A chromosome 21 condition. Health needs, development, and features vary from person to person.

Read NIH MedlinePlus Genetics

Turner Syndrome

An X chromosome condition that can affect growth, heart health, and ovarian function. Features vary.

Read NIH MedlinePlus Genetics

Klinefelter Syndrome

Usually involves an extra X chromosome (47,XXY). Features vary and can include hormone, learning, or fertility concerns.

Read NIH MedlinePlus Genetics

Edwards Syndrome (Trisomy 18)

Also called trisomy 18. It can affect growth and multiple organ systems; presentation and care needs vary.
